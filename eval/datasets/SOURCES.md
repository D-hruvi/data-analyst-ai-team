# Dataset Sources

All three evaluation datasets come from the public `mwaskom/seaborn-data` repository on GitHub: https://github.com/mwaskom/seaborn-data

- `tips.csv` — restaurant bills and tips (244 rows, 7 columns).
- `penguins.csv` — penguin measurements, including missing values (344 rows, 7 columns).
- `taxis.csv` — New York taxi rides, including dates and missing values (6,433 rows, 14 columns).


`sample_sales.csv` is a small, made-up dataset used in Stages 1 and 2. It is not part of the evaluation.
## Licence status (checked 2026-10-27)

No licence for the data files could be established. The seaborn-data repository describes itself as a
download target for seaborn examples, says datasets may change or be removed, and says some were modified
from their original sources. It lists penguins as coming from Allison Horst's penguins project.
This project has not verified the origin or licence of tips.csv or taxis.csv.
The copies here are small samples used for educational evaluation only. If an owner objects, they will be removed.
Anyone reusing them should check the upstream sources.
